Nvidia live updates: Stock tanks after new affair allegations, board reportedly preparing to oust Jensen Huang
NVDA -4.01%
Nvidia ( NVDA ) stock tanked in after-hours trading following a report from the New York Times that embattled Nvidia ( NVDA ) CEO Jensen Huang was allegedly involved in a second extramarital affair.
The company's board is reportedly meeting to determine if it will oust Huang as chief executive, the Wall Street Journal reports .
According to the report, Huang, whose relationship with a woman alleged to be a Chinese spy has embroiled the company in scandal this week, also had a relationship with Nvidia executive Kimberly Powell.
Nvidia stock fell in after-hours trading following the report.
The Times report says that Huang and Powell were seen being affectionate at a hotel during a company conference in March.
Huang is already under investigation for an affair with Alice Huang, an alleged Chinese spy. The CEO admitted the affair in a memo to employees.
"I was unfaithful to my wife. My actions hurt her and our children. There is no excuse for what I did. I am deeply sorry," Huang wrote.
The Information reported on Tuesday that Alice Huang is believed to be involved in a network of Chinese companies that facilitated the shipment of $7.5 billion ‌in chip sales to China.
Given the size and importance of Nvidia in the stock market today, the revelation marks one of the biggest CEO-personal conduct scandals in history . It also highlights Nvidia's key-person risk and uncertain succession plan as questions swirl about whether the board will ask Huang to step down.
The news has drawn immediate and varied reactions from leaders ranging from the US president to other CEOs. President Trump stood by Huang, calling him "an honorable guy," but other members of the president's party are expected to put more scrutiny on the company.
Trump also stated on Monday that the FBI was looking into Huang's relationship.
In a statement on Sunday, Nvidia's board said it has "no reason to believe that either national security or Nvidia secrets were compromised."
